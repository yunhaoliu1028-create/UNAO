CCC estimate有两个常见format：
第一种“Estimate” or “Final Bill” 通常第一页会写
里面的operation列通常不会缩写
Remove/Replace
Repair
Remove/Install
Blend
Parts Type列和Type(column behind Labor)会有缩写，这是他们的解释：
T = Taxable Item, RPD = Related Prior Damage, AA = Appearance Allowance, UPD = Unrelated Prior Damage, PDR = Paintless Dent Repair, A/M = Aftermarket, Rechr = Rechromed, Reman =
Remanufactured, OEM = New Original Equipment Manufacturer, Recor = Re-cored, RECOND = Reconditioned, LKQ = Like Kind Quality or Used, Diag = Diagnostic, Elec = Electrical, Mech =
Mechanical, Ref = Refinish, Struc = Structural


第二种 “Estimate of Record” “Preliminary Estimate” “Supplement of Record xx with Summary” ““Preliminary Supplement xx with Summary”
Adj.=Adjacent.

""CURE TIME"" MEANS THE LENGTH OF TIME THAT, PER THE ADHESIVE
MANUFACTURER, THE WINDSHIELD ADHESIVE NEEDS TO CURE UNTIL THE WINDSHIELD CAN PROPERLY FUNCTION AS A SAFETY DEVICE PURSUANT

请注意，在第二种format估价下，尤其是第一页标题包含 “with Summary” 代表这个估价包含了前几页的总估价，再加上一个SUPPLEMENT SUMMARY，请务必只读取总估价避免overlap.


所有的估价都是从上往下读，主要信息是从Oper(operation), Description和Line Notes提取，有时你会看到rpr rocker mldg 0.3body hour but without refinish time, with a notes saying"drop," that mean partially R/I, its not for repair&refinish.类似的还有rpr qtr glass 0.3b with notes "rope," rpr w'shield with note "rope," etc..请务必理解。
